Privacy policy

How we handle the information you share with us. Effective November 12, 2026.

Who we are

Fixit4U is a phone, tablet, laptop, game console and smartwatch repair shop at 1111 S College St, Ste 200 A, Auburn, AL 36832. You can reach us at (334) 849-0404 or info@thefixit4you.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through the forms on this website:

  • Quote and appointment requests: your name, phone number, email address, device brand and model, the repair you need, your description of the problem, and your preferred date and time.
  • Call-back requests: your name, phone number, the best time to call, and an optional note about the problem.

We also collect:

  • "Ask us" chat: if you type into the chat assistant, the conversation is emailed to us when you leave the page, along with the page you were on, the time, whether you used a phone or computer, and the website that sent you here. We use it to answer questions and improve our replies. Please don't type personal details you don't want us to have.
  • Website statistics: we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used, your approximate city and region (based on your internet connection), your type of device and browser, and actions like tapping Call, Text, Directions or sending a form. It doesn't tell us who you are.

We don't ask for payment details, passwords or government ID numbers on this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, give you a price and schedule your repair.
  • To call or email you about your repair.
  • To send you a confirmation that we received your request.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to send marketing messages.

Services that help run this website

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form submissions and chat conversations to our email inbox and sends your confirmation email.
  • Google Analytics (Google) collects the website statistics described above using cookies. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.
  • Google provides the map, the fonts and, where shown, our Google reviews. When these load, Google may receive basic technical information such as your IP address, and the map may set its own cookies.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

This website doesn't use advertising cookies. Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits. The site may also store small, temporary settings in your browser, such as cached reviews, to load pages faster. The embedded Google map may set cookies of its own.

How long we keep it

We keep your request in our email for as long as we need it to help you and keep a record of your repair. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us. Call (334) 849-0404 or email info@thefixit4you.com.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
