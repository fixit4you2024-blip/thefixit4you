Buying & selling

Good to know before you come in

Still have a question? Call (334) 849-0404 or walk in.

What do you buy?

Phones, tablets, laptops, game consoles and smartwatches, including devices that are cracked, carrier-locked or won't turn on.

How do you pay?

Cash or Zelle. You can also trade in your device for credit.

What do I need to bring?

Your device and a photo ID. A photo ID is required to sell.

Do you buy broken or locked phones?

Yes. We buy phones with cracked screens, phones that won't turn on and carrier-locked phones. The offer depends on the model and its condition.

Do the phones you sell have a warranty?

Yes. We sell new, used and refurbished phones, and they come with a 30-day technical warranty.

Can I trade in my old phone?

Yes. Trade-in is available. Ask in store or call (334) 849-0404 for details.
