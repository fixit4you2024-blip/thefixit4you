Pricing questions

Straight answers before you visit

Every quote is free. Don't see your device? Send a quote request or call (334) 849-0404.

Why do repair prices vary?

The price depends on your exact model and the part it needs. Newer and larger models usually cost more, and for iPhone repairs it also depends on the part you choose: aftermarket or genuine Apple. iPhone screens come as aftermarket LCD, aftermarket OLED or genuine Apple.

Is the price confirmed before you start?

Yes. Quotes are free. We check your device and confirm the exact price with you before starting any repair.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Students, military, veterans and first responders, returning customers, social media followers, repair bundles and multi-device visits save $5 to $10. Screen repairs come with a free or half-price screen protector, and trade-ins earn store credit. See all discounts.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Repairs come with a 90-day technical warranty.

How long does a repair take?

Phone screens usually take 10–15 minutes and batteries 15–25 minutes. Cameras take about 35–55 minutes, and charging ports and back glass over an hour. Water damage, tablets, laptops and consoles usually take 1–2 days, and some are done the same day. We'll tell you the time before we start.

Can I sell my old phone?

Yes. Tell us the model and its condition and we'll make you an offer, paid in cash or Zelle. Cracked, locked or dead phones are fine. Bring a photo ID to sell.
