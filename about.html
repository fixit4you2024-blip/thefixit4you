Our story

A repair shop Auburn can walk into

Fixit4U opened on South College Street in 2024, built on 4 years of repairing phones, tablets and laptops. The idea was simple: give people in Auburn a shop where they can walk in, talk to the person fixing their device, and know the price before any work begins.

Most repairs are done the same day, usually in about 30 minutes, and they come with a 90-day technical warranty. We also buy and sell phones, so a cracked screen or an upgrade can be sorted in one visit.
